Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

FittMatch ("we", "us", or "our") operates the FittMatch mobile application and the website fittmatch.fit (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

This policy describes our data practices. Where consent is required, we will request it separately. Our Terms of Service govern your use of the Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, as well as information generated through your use of the Service:

  • Account and sign-in information: email address, name, and role (coach or client). Email signup and sign-in use one-time email codes rather than an app password or a login link. If you choose Apple or Google for signup or sign-in, we receive an authentication token and the account information the provider makes available, such as an identifier, email address, and name. Apple may supply a private relay email address. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile and listing data: for coaches — profile photos and captions, introduction videos, professional background, certifications, supporting certificate documents, specialties, availability, and rate expectations; for clients — company or facility details, logos, photos, website, locations, and job listings, including role requirements, pay ranges, and schedules.
  • Location and search information: profile and job cities, location coordinates associated with selected locations, additional cities, search areas, filters, and saved searches. We use this information to show relevant profiles and opportunities.
  • Activity and communications: likes, passes, matches, message requests, messages and attachments, reviews, hiring or application status, notification preferences, and safety reports.
  • Device and technical data: app activity, device and session metadata, diagnostic information, and push notification tokens used to deliver notifications.
  • Subscription data: purchase history and entitlement status managed through the Apple App Store and RevenueCat. We do not store raw payment card details.
  • Local preferences: before sign-in, the app stores your coach/client selection on your device so the choice can carry into signup and onboarding. The selection can be changed before sign-in.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you, send sign-in and email-change codes, and create and maintain your account and profile.
  • To power the swipe-and-match discovery experience.
  • To facilitate likes, message requests, conversations, reviews, and hiring activity, including introductions before a mutual connection where the app permits them.
  • To send notifications according to your preferences and provide saved-search email updates where enabled. Authentication and essential account messages are separate from optional notifications.
  • To process subscription purchases and verify entitlements.
  • To understand feature use, improve the Service, and monitor, diagnose, and fix technical issues.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service, investigate reports of misconduct, and keep the platform safe — which may include reviewing account activity, match history, and message content where there is a reasonable basis to suspect a violation.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party providers, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and serverless edge functions. Data is stored in Supabase-managed PostgreSQL instances.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase receipts and entitlement status.
  • Expo / Expo Push Notification Service — cross-platform push notification delivery.
  • PostHog — product analytics and error reporting where configured. Events may include account identifiers, feature interactions, device and session metadata, and error details.
  • Apple and Google — optional signup and sign-in providers. Their own privacy policies govern information they collect through their authentication services.
  • Email delivery providers — delivery of authentication codes, account messages, and saved-search updates, using your email address and the message content.
  • Website hosting providers — hosting the website and share-link landing pages, including GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser metadata.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • In discovery and shared links: profile and job information can be visible before a match. Completed coach profiles are discoverable while “Open to offers” is enabled; completed client profiles and eligible live job listings may also be discoverable. Share links can open the app and, where available, public pages or previews accessible without signing in. Public presentation information can include names, cities, profile text, photos, videos, professional qualifications, visible reviews and reviewer names, company details, and job information. Public web profile responses exclude login email addresses, precise coordinates, private certificate documents, messages, and account-security data.
  • Public media: profile photos and coach introduction videos are served from public media URLs. Do not upload confidential information to these fields. Certificate proof documents are stored separately for authorised review and are not part of public profile pages.
  • With conversation participants: messages, attachments, and introductions are shared with their recipients. A mutual match is not required for every permitted message request.
  • With service providers: as described in Section 4, to operate the Service.
  • For legal compliance: if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality obligations.

Coaches can turn off “Open to offers” in Settings to hide their profile from discovery and supported public profile pages. Clients can manage the status of their listings. These controls do not revoke copies already saved by other people, cached previews, or direct media URLs; contact us if you need help removing previously published material.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You can request deletion of your account and personal data. Information needed for legal compliance, security, or dispute resolution may be retained for those purposes.

You can request account deletion in the app’s Settings. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Some records may remain with payment providers, in limited backups, or where needed for legal compliance, security, or dispute resolution. Uploaded media may require separate removal; contact privacy@fittmatch.fit for help with remaining uploads after account deletion. We cannot remove copies independently retained by recipients or third-party sharing services.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Data portability.

You can edit profile information, change notification preferences, manage saved searches, and request account deletion in the app. To exercise other rights or request assistance, contact us at privacy@fittmatch.fit. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

FittMatch is intended for adults aged 18 and over, as stated in our Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS), row-level security policies on our database, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before applying a material change.
