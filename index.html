How It Works

Hired in three steps

No résumé uploads, no cover letters. Just a profile, a swipe, and a conversation.

1

Create Your Profile

Coaches add their certifications, specialties, and rate expectations. Gyms and studios post their open positions and culture.

2

Swipe & Match

Coaches swipe on job listings. Gyms swipe on coaches. A mutual right-swipe creates a match — no one-sided awkwardness.

3

Start the Conversation

Every match unlocks a private message thread. Agree on terms, schedule a trial session, and get to work.
