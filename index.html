How It Works

From discovery to your next fit

Show what makes you a great fit, connect with the right people, and move the conversation forward.

1

Create Your Profile

Coaches showcase certifications, specialties, photos, and an intro video. Gyms and studios share their culture and post jobs with hourly or salary pay.

2

Swipe & Match

Browse jobs or coaches by location and specialty. Like a promising fit, save your search, and find mutual matches or send a message request.

3

Connect & Keep Moving

Share jobs and propose interviews in chat, then follow hiring progress through to an offer. Coaches message free; gyms and studios need Client Pro to send messages.

Features

Built for fitness, not generic hiring

Everything the industry needs, nothing it doesn't.

Discovery That Fits

Find coaches by specialty and experience, or jobs by facility type and minimum pay. Search by location, including gyms and listings with multiple cities.

Saved Searches & Weekly Updates

Save your favorite filters and return to them anytime. Opt into weekly email digests to hear about new coaches or jobs that match your searches.

Profiles with Personality

Bring your experience to life with photos, certifications, and a coach intro video. Photo and video uploads keep going while you explore the app.

More Than a Message

Connect through mutual matches or message requests you can accept or decline. Share photos and job listings, react to messages, and propose interviews right in chat.

A Pipeline for Every Job

Keep applicants organized from new application to interview, offer, and hire. Gyms can add private notes, while coaches see their application status and receive stage-change notifications.

Updates You Control

Stay up to date on matches, messages, and hiring progress. Choose your notification preferences and clear notifications individually or several at once.

Client Pro for Gyms & Studios

Unlock unlimited daily swipes, send messages to coaches, and see your full applicant list. Keep recruiting moving from the first introduction to your next hire.

Optional Coach Pro

Discover and message for free. Upgrade to see which gyms are interested in you and use Super Likes to highlight the opportunities you want most.

Ready to find your fit?

Download FittMatch on iOS and start swiping today. Discovery and messaging are free for coaches, with optional Coach Pro extras.
